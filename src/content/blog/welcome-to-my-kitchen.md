I've always believed that cooking is one of the most loving things you can do. Not just for others, but for yourself.

When I started this blog, I wanted to create a space where I could share the recipes that have shaped my life — the ones I grew up with, the ones I've discovered along the way, and the ones I've created myself.

## What to Expect

You'll find recipes here that are:

- **Approachable** — No fancy equipment or impossible-to-find ingredients
- **Delicious** — Everything is tested multiple times in my own kitchen
- **Made with love** — Because that's really what it's all about

## My Cooking Philosophy

I don't believe in strict rules in the kitchen. Cooking should be joyful, creative, and deeply personal. If a recipe calls for basil and you only have cilantro — go for it. The best meals come from experimentation and intuition.

> "Cooking is like love. It should be entered into with abandon or not at all." — Harriet Van Horne
