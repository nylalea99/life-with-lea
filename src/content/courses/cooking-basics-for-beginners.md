# Cooking Basics for Beginners

Welcome to the course! This is designed for anyone who wants to feel more confident in the kitchen. No prior experience needed.

## What You'll Learn

- How to stock your kitchen with essential tools and ingredients
- Basic knife skills and food safety
- How to read and follow a recipe
- Fundamental cooking techniques (sautéing, roasting, boiling, etc.)
- How to season food properly
- Time-saving tips for busy weeknights
- How to meal prep like a pro
- Building your own recipes from scratch

## Who This Is For

- Complete beginners who want to learn to cook
- Anyone who feels intimidated in the kitchen
- People who want to eat better but don't know where to start

This course is completely free because I believe everyone deserves to know how to feed themselves well.
