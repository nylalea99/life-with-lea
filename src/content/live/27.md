## Why 27 Matters

No one understands why 27 means so much to me. But it proves ten years since my last ten year plan. Six years since shitty friendships. Twenty-seven years of crying in closets and bathrooms to having built a life for myself.

To being 27 and happy, when there were moments I didn't think I deserved it.

## 2024 Accomplishments

- Moved to a new country alone
- Celebrated a birthday without the people I've celebrated all previous birthdays with
- Survived falling down the stairs (again)
- Started a new job (twice with the same company)
- Built a relationship with four vendors
- Looked after a household — cleaning, cooking, all of it
- Visited my home country
- Successfully explained my concussion story in Portuguese
- Reached the one year mark of my concussion and car accident

To 2024 and all the hours it brought.
