I used to feel guilty when I wasn't doing something productive. Rest felt like wasted time. Sitting on the couch without checking something off my list felt like failure.

But I've come to realize that doing nothing is actually doing something important: **resting.**

## Why We Need Rest

Our brains aren't designed to be "on" 24/7. Constant stimulation — emails, social media, news, to-do lists — keeps us in a state of chronic stress. And chronic stress doesn't just make us tired; it affects our health, our creativity, and our relationships.

## What "Doing Nothing" Looks Like for Me

- Sitting with my morning coffee for 20 minutes (no phone!)
- Taking a walk without a destination or a podcast
- Lying in the grass and watching clouds
- Reading fiction purely for pleasure
- Just... sitting

## The Guilt Factor

If you're like me, you might feel guilty at first. That's normal. We've been conditioned to equate busyness with worth. But here's the truth: **you are not what you produce.**

You are allowed to rest. You are allowed to take up space without justifying it with productivity.

## A Challenge

Try this: for one week, carve out 15 minutes each day to do absolutely nothing. No phone, no book, no agenda. Just be.

See what happens. I think you might be surprised.
