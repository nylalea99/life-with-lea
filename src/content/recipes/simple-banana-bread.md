There's something deeply comforting about a warm slice of banana bread. This is the recipe I've been making for years — it's simple, reliable, and always delicious.

## Ingredients

- 3 ripe bananas, mashed
- 1/3 cup melted butter
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- Pinch of salt
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour

## Instructions

1. **Preheat** your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x5 loaf pan.

2. **Mash the bananas** in a large bowl. Stir in the melted butter.

3. **Mix in** the sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract.

4. **Add** the baking soda and salt, then gently fold in the flour until just combined. Don't over-mix!

5. **Pour** the batter into your prepared loaf pan.

6. **Bake** for 55-60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

7. **Cool** in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.

## My Favorite Variations

- **Chocolate chip:** Fold in 1/2 cup chocolate chips before baking
- **Walnuts:** Add 1/2 cup chopped walnuts for crunch
- **Cinnamon swirl:** Add a teaspoon of cinnamon to half the batter and swirl it through
