This honey garlic lemon chicken is one of my absolute favorite weeknight dinners. It comes together in under 30 minutes, uses just one pan, and tastes like you spent way more time on it than you did.

## Ingredients

- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 3 tablespoons honey
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley for garnish

## Instructions

1. **Prep the chicken:** Season chicken breasts with salt and pepper on both sides.

2. **Make the sauce:** In a small bowl, whisk together honey, soy sauce, minced garlic, and lemon juice.

3. **Sear the chicken:** Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken for 5-6 minutes per side until golden brown.

4. **Add the sauce:** Pour the sauce over the chicken, reduce heat to medium-low, and let it simmer for 5-7 minutes until the sauce thickens and the chicken is cooked through.

5. **Serve:** Garnish with fresh parsley and serve with rice or roasted vegetables.

## Tips

- **Don't overcook the chicken.** Use a meat thermometer — chicken is done at 165°F.
- **Make it a meal:** Serve over fluffy jasmine rice with steamed broccoli.
- **Meal prep friendly:** Stores well in the fridge for up to 3 days.
